## This is the largest single reconstruction task in the whole recovery (RECOVERY.md section 12, item 4)

Everything below is **NOT in the jar** and must be built from RECOVERY.md
section 7's spec, in full, from scratch:
- The `DecayPhase` 6-phase enum (`HOLD_FRESH → BUILD → HOLD_FULL_SHROUD →
  EROSION → HOLD_FULL_SKELETON → ASH`) with per-phase config-driven durations.
- The four rendering lessons (never two overlapping transparent draw calls
  for a crossfade — use `BlendedTextureCache` instead; `ModelPart.visible`
  needs a scale-to-zero ramp, not a hard boolean flip; only rotate a model
  part on one axis, not `xRot`+`zRot` together; `GraveMarkerRenderer` must
  extend `LivingEntityRenderer`, not `EntityRenderer`, for the auto Y-flip).
- The Ash-Phase texture/pose/collapse-curve system
  (`generate_ash_texture.py`, `ashCollapseCurve()`, `ashPoseT()`,
  `ASH_HIDE_LIMBS_AT`/`ASH_HIDE_HEAD_AT`/`ASH_HIDE_BODY_AT`).
- `PlayerGraveMarkerEntity` (needs `EntityAttributeCreationEvent`
  registration or it NPEs silently at construction — doesn't show at
  compile time).
- 4 known open bugs at time of loss (BUILD-phase jump, skeleton→ash
  darkening pacing, "weird" shrink, and the priority one: shared
  `wideModel`/`slimModel` singleton `PlayerModel` state bleeding decay
  state between simultaneous corpses — rendering state must be held
  per-entity, never on the model object).

RECOVERY.md itself recommends writing **one comprehensive fix prompt** for
items 1–4 plus the two grave-marker wishes, rather than incremental steps —
worth keeping that plan when this mod's turn comes up.
